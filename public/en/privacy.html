Privacy policy

We only collect what you send us through the contact form, and only to reply to you.

Who we are

The controller of your personal data is: Markopoulos Olive Mill, Malesina 350 01, Fthiotida, Greece.

Phone: +30 22330 51298. Email: info@markopoulos-oliveoil.gr.

What we collect

Only what you fill in on the contact form: your name, phone number or email, quantity of olives (if given) and your message. Like every web server, our hosting provider logs technical visit data (such as IP addresses) for security.

Why we use it

To reply to your message and arrange our service for you. The legal basis is your consent (Article 6(1)(a) of the General Data Protection Regulation) and steps you ask us to take before an agreement (Article 6(1)(b)).

How long we keep it

As long as needed to deal with your request and no longer than 12 months, unless we end up working together and the law requires us to keep records for longer (for example, for tax purposes).

Who we share it with

We do not sell your details or pass them on for advertising. Messages pass through our web hosting and email provider, which processes them only on our behalf.

Cookies and the map

This website uses no cookies and no analytics or advertising tools. Fonts load from our own server. The Google map on the contact page loads only if you press “Show map”; from then on, Google’s privacy policy applies.

Your rights

You can ask for access to your data, its correction or deletion, restriction of or objection to its processing, and you can withdraw your consent at any time. Write to us or call us using the details above. If you believe we have not respected your rights, you can contact the Hellenic Data Protection Authority (www.dpa.gr).
