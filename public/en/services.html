Services for olive growers

We work for private growers from Malesina and the surrounding area, with every olive variety, from October to January.

  • Milling batch by batch

    Each batch is processed on its own and never mixed with another grower’s fruit. The oil you take home carries the character of your own trees.

  • Cold extraction below 27°C

    The paste is malaxed at a controlled temperature below 27°C, and the oil is separated in a stainless-steel centrifuge. The low temperature protects the oil’s aromas.

    This is exactly what “cold extraction” means under EU olive oil rules.

  • Lab analysis

    Every batch comes with a laboratory analysis of acidity (free fatty acids) and polyphenols, so you know what oil you have, whether it is for your kitchen or for sale.

  • Storage

    You can leave your oil with us in closed stainless-steel tanks and collect it whenever suits you.

How it works

  1. Book a slot

    Call +30 22330 51298 and we agree a day and time.

  2. Bring your olives

    We weigh them and keep them apart as your own batch.

  3. We mill

    Your batch runs through the line with cold extraction below 27°C.

  4. Take your oil

    In your own containers or stored with us, together with its analysis.

Common questions

How much does milling cost?

The milling fee is set at the start of each season. Call us on +30 22330 51298 for the current rate and payment options.

Will I get oil only from my own olives?

Yes. Every batch is milled separately and never mixed with another grower’s fruit.

Is there a minimum quantity?

Tell us on the phone how much fruit you have, and we will tell you how we can mill your batch.

How long will I wait?

With a booked slot you know in advance when your olives will be milled. At the height of the season, call a few days ahead.

How soon after picking should I bring the olives?

As soon as you can, ideally within one or two days. Olives left in sacks warm up and the acidity of the oil rises. Use ventilated crates and keep the fruit somewhere cool and shaded.

What does the analysis show?

The acidity (free fatty acids) and the polyphenols of your oil. If you need anything more, ask us.

Can I leave my oil with you?

Yes, in closed stainless-steel tanks. Ask us about duration and cost.

Which olive varieties do you mill?

Every olive variety grown in the area.

When are you open?

During the season (October – January): Every day, 07:00 – 20:00.

I own olive trees in the area but live abroad. Can I still use the mill?

Write to us in English through the contact form with roughly how much fruit you expect and when, and we will arrange the milling with you.
